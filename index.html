01 — About

I build AI that ships.

B.Tech CS at KIIT. I build multi-agent AI systems, LLMOps tooling, and full-stack products, and I take them from idea to production.

I founded AakaarIO, an AI-powered software operating platform. It takes a product from idea to production, then keeps maintaining it, all in one place: its agents plan the architecture, generate the frontend and backend, connect databases and APIs, deploy the app, and keep helping maintain it after launch.

Its multi-agent orchestration is the subject of a patent I'm writing now. Beyond building software, ORION runs the business around it: CRM, business intelligence, automation and workflows.

01

Sentinel

LLMOps · Aug 2026 – now

Closed-loop evaluation and optimisation for LLM and RAG apps.

  • Evaluates RAG, LLM and agentic apps, diagnoses failures from evidence, and proposes fixes that are tested in an isolated sandbox first.
  • Quality gates for regressions, safety, cost and latency, with LLM-as-Judge scoring calibrated against human review and RBAC-gated promotion to production.
  • Full architecture written up: HLD/LLD, DFD levels 0–2, ER model, Celery async pipelines, vector-based failure clustering, and drift-triggered re-evaluation in CI/CD.

03 — How LLM evals work

Evals, not guesswork.

Changing a prompt or a model without evals is guessing. This is the loop I build into Sentinel: a fixed test set, automatic judges checked against people, and a gate that decides whether a change ships. Click a stage, then run an eval yourself.
